Our Story

Born from a Passion for Mediterranean Flavors

Cafe Olé was born from a deep love of Mediterranean cuisine and a desire to share those vibrant flavors with the people of Philadelphia. Nestled in the heart of Old City — one of Philly's most historic and charming neighborhoods — we've created a warm, welcoming space where great food meets great company.

Our menu draws inspiration from the sun-drenched kitchens of Israel, Morocco, and the greater Mediterranean region. The centerpiece of our kitchen is the iconic shakshuka — eggs poached in richly spiced tomato sauce, served with artisan bread made fresh every morning. It's a dish that captures everything we believe in: simplicity, quality, and soul.

Every ingredient that enters our kitchen is chosen with care. Our hummus is made in-house. Our bread is baked fresh daily. Our shawarma is slow-roasted with care and love. We believe that food should be honest, nourishing, and deeply satisfying.

Our Neighborhood

In the Heart of Old City

Old City Philadelphia is one of America's most historic neighborhoods — home to Independence Hall, the Liberty Bell, and centuries of American history. It's also a vibrant, artistic community filled with galleries, boutiques, restaurants, and creative spaces.

Our cafe at 147 N 3rd Street sits right in the center of this energy. We're a short walk from some of Philly's most beloved landmarks and surrounded by an incredible community of residents, artists, tourists, and professionals who make up our daily family of guests.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick coffee before touring the historic district, or settling in for a long Mediterranean brunch with friends, Cafe Olé is your home base in Old City.
