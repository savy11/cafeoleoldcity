About Us

Located at 147 N 3rd St, Cafe Ole is a cozy neighborhood eatery with a warm, inviting atmosphere and brightly colored walls. Whether you're craving our famous House Special Shakshuka, a rich Nutella Latte, or fresh Mediterranean platters, we have something for everyone. We also offer great outdoor seating and are dog-friendly!
