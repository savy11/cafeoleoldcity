Our Story

A Taste of the Mediterranean in Old City

Nestled in the charming historic streets of Old City Philadelphia, Cafe Olé brings the vibrant flavors of the Mediterranean to your table. From the moment you step through our doors, you're transported to the sun-drenched cafes of Israel, Morocco, and the Middle East.

We take pride in every dish — from our legendary shakshuka, prepared fresh in cast iron with eggs poached to perfection, to our slow-roasted shawarma and house-made hummus. Every meal is crafted with love, using the freshest ingredients.
